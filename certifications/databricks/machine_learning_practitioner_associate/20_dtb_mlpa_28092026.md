### Monitoring MLOps Solutions

#### Four-Dimensions of Model Monitoring

- **Business Requirements**
  - ensure the ML solution aligns with and fullfills specific business requirements
  - ensure continued relevance to evolving business needs
- **Model Performance**
  - track accuracy and efficiency of the model over time
  - detecting and addressing types of drift or degradation
- **Resource utilization**
  - ensure efficient utilizatio within the ML infrastructure
  - compliance with SLAs for system performance and availability
- **Traceability**
  - facilitate audit tracks for troubleshooting regulatory compliance, and model improvement
  - tracking data lineage, to understand the origin movement, and transformation of data
- accuracy alone is not enough - monitoring must span all four dimensions
  - solutions is relevant, accurate, efficient, and accountable throughout its production life

#### The Four Types of Drift

- drift is the gradual mismatch between the world a model was trained on and the world it now operates in
- **data drift**
  - statistical properties of input data change over time $\to$ changes in the shape of distribution
  - introduces inconsistencies in the data patterns
- **concept drift**
  - changes in the function that maps to the outputs 
  - the same features predicts a different outcomes 
  - dependencies on inputs
  - changes in the relationships between input features and the tagrt variable
  - forces the model to adapt to new patterns to stay relevant
- **model quality drift**
  - decrease in the model is predictive performance over time
  - detected through worsening metrics
- **bias drift**
  - prediction accuracy diverse across groups as their distribution shifts
  - shifts in model outcomes that could lead to unfair treatment of certain groups
  - crucial to maintain fairness and ethical standards in model predictions
- a model trained on a symmetric distribution will have performance issues once the production data becomes skewed (this is data drift)
  - if the inputs are similar, but the quality degrades, this is a sign of model quality drift
- bias group occurs when there is a shift in the group distribution in production to the training data
  - emergence of a new group or a feature that *has limited data for a small group of customers*
