# Inglês · Duolingo — Nível 32

> **Tema oficial:** Forme o negativo do tempo presente · **Seção 2, Unidade 22** · Curso: Inglês para falantes de português

## Visão geral

Neste nível você forma **frases negativas no presente simples**. Com verbos comuns, usa-se o auxiliar **`do not` / `does not`** (contraídos `don't` / `doesn't`) + **verbo na forma base**. Com o verbo **`to be`**, basta acrescentar `not` (`am not`, `is not` / `isn't`, `are not` / `aren't`). Também entram `never`, `no`, `nobody`, `nothing`, perguntas negativas simples e respostas curtas negativas.

```text
I don't like coffee. She doesn't drink tea.                     →  Eu não gosto de café. Ela não toma chá.
They don't work on Sundays.                                      →  Eles não trabalham aos domingos.
He isn't at home. We aren't tired.                               →  Ele não está em casa. Nós não estamos cansados.
```

Ideias centrais:

1. `I / you / we / they` + **`don't`** + verbo base; `he / she / it` + **`doesn't`** + verbo base.
2. Depois de `doesn't`, **sem -s no verbo**: `She doesn't work`, não `She doesn't works`.
3. Com `to be`, não se usa `do`: `He isn't`, nunca `He doesn't is`.

## Gramática em exemplos

### 1. Estrutura geral da negativa

| Sujeito | Auxiliar negativo | Verbo base | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | do not (don't) | like | I don't like tea. |
| you | do not (don't) | speak | You don't speak Italian. |
| he / she / it | does not (doesn't) | work | She doesn't work here. |
| we | do not (don't) | live | We don't live here. |
| they | do not (don't) | play | They don't play football. |

- Fórmula: **sujeito + do / does + not + verbo base**.

### 2. Não existe `-s` depois de `doesn't`

| Afirmativa | Negativa |
|---|---|
| She works. | She doesn't work. |
| He likes it. | He doesn't like it. |
| It rains. | It doesn't rain. |
| My dad watches TV. | My dad doesn't watch TV. |
| The bus stops here. | The bus doesn't stop here. |

- O `-s` da terceira pessoa passa para o auxiliar: `does` já carrega a marca de terceira pessoa.

### 3. Verbos com `-es` e `-ies` na afirmativa e a volta à base

| Afirmativa | Negativa |
|---|---|
| He goes. | He doesn't go. |
| She watches. | She doesn't watch. |
| He studies. | He doesn't study. |
| She has a car. | She doesn't have a car. |
| It fixes things. | It doesn't fix things. |

- `has` volta para `have`; `goes` volta para `go`; `studies` volta para `study`.

### 4. A negativa do verbo `to be`

| Sujeito | Forma completa | Contraída |
|---|---|---|
| I | I am not | I'm not |
| you | you are not | you aren't / you're not |
| he / she / it | is not | isn't / he's not |
| we | we are not | we aren't / we're not |
| they | they are not | they aren't / they're not |

- Não use `do` com `to be`: `She isn't happy`, nunca `She doesn't be happy`.
- Não existe `amn't` em inglês padrão: use `I'm not`.

### 5. Contrações

| Completa | Contraída |
|---|---|
| do not | don't |
| does not | doesn't |
| is not | isn't |
| are not | aren't |
| cannot / can not | can't |
| will not | won't |

- A forma contraída é comum na fala; a completa dá ênfase: `I do not agree.`

### 6. `Never` e outros advérbios

| Frase | Observação |
|---|---|
| I never eat meat. | `never` já é negativa; **sem** `don't` |
| She never calls me. | sem `doesn't` |
| He rarely drinks coffee. | `rarely` = raramente |
| We don't often go out. | `often` depois de `don't` |
| They don't always agree. | `always` depois de `don't` |

- **Dupla negação** não é usada: `I never eat meat`, não `I don't never eat meat`.
- `never` vem antes do verbo principal e depois do verbo `to be`: `He never works. She is never late.`

### 7. `Any`, `no`, `nobody`, `nothing`

| Com `not` | Com palavra negativa | Tradução |
|---|---|---|
| I don't have any money. | I have no money. | Não tenho dinheiro. |
| I don't know anybody. | I know nobody. | Não conheço ninguém. |
| I don't see anything. | I see nothing. | Não vejo nada. |
| She doesn't go anywhere. | She goes nowhere. | Ela não vai a lugar nenhum. |

- Prefira a primeira coluna (`not … any`), mais natural; as duas dizem a mesma coisa.

### 8. Respostas curtas negativas

| Pergunta | Resposta curta |
|---|---|
| Do you like fish? | No, I don't. |
| Does he live here? | No, he doesn't. |
| Do they work today? | No, they don't. |
| Is she tired? | No, she isn't. |
| Are you a teacher? | No, I'm not. |

- A resposta curta repete o auxiliar da pergunta, não o verbo principal.

### 9. `Don't` com imperativo e com verbos de opinião

| Frase | Tradução |
|---|---|
| Don't worry. | Não se preocupe. |
| Don't be late. | Não se atrase. |
| I don't think so. | Acho que não. |
| I don't think he knows. | Acho que ele não sabe. |
| I don't want to talk. | Não quero conversar. |

- Em `I don't think he knows`, a negativa fica no verbo `think` (em português seria "penso que ele não sabe").

### 10. Afirmativa x negativa: resumo comparativo

| Afirmativa | Negativa |
|---|---|
| I like fish. | I don't like fish. |
| You speak French. | You don't speak French. |
| She lives here. | She doesn't live here. |
| We work on Saturdays. | We don't work on Saturdays. |
| They have a dog. | They don't have a dog. |
| He is tall. | He isn't tall. |
| I am ready. | I'm not ready. |

## Diálogos

### Diálogo 1 — Preferências

- Ana: Do you like coffee?
- Ben: No, I don't. I drink tea.
- Ana: Does your wife drink coffee?
- Ben: No, she doesn't. She doesn't like it either.

### Diálogo 2 — Rotina

- Mia: Do you work on Saturdays?
- Leo: No, I don't. My office doesn't open on weekends.
- Mia: And your brother?
- Leo: He doesn't work on Saturdays either, but he works on Sundays.

### Diálogo 3 — Sem entender

- Tourist: Excuse me, do you speak English?
- Local: A little. I don't speak it very well.
- Tourist: That's fine. I don't understand Portuguese.
- Local: Don't worry. I can help you.

## Pontos de atenção e erros comuns

- **`Doesn't` + verbo base**: `She doesn't work`, não `She doesn't works`.
- **`Don't` x `doesn't`**: `don't` com `I / you / we / they`; `doesn't` com `he / she / it`.
- **Nunca use `do` com `to be`**: `He isn't`, não `He doesn't is`.
- **Sem dupla negação**: `I never see anyone` ou `I don't see anyone`, não `I don't never see anybody`.
- **O verbo principal não vai para a negativa sem auxiliar**: `I like not tea` e `I no like tea` estão errados.
- **`No` x `not`**: `no` vem antes de substantivo (`no money`); `not` nega o verbo ou adjetivo (`not tired`).
- **`Have` como posse**: `I don't have a car` é a forma padrão; no inglês britânico também se ouve `I haven't got a car`.
- **Resposta curta**: `No, I don't`, não `No, I don't like` (mantém-se só o auxiliar).
- **Pronúncia**: `don't` /doʊnt/ e `doesn't` /ˈdʌzənt/; a segunda tem duas sílabas.

## Complete as lacunas

1. She ____ like tea. (don't / doesn't) → `doesn't`
2. I ____ speak German. (don't / doesn't) → `don't`
3. He doesn't ____ here. (work / works) → `work`
4. They ____ at home. (aren't / don't) → `aren't`
5. My dad ____ drink coffee. (doesn't / isn't) → `doesn't`
6. I ____ eat meat. (never / don't never) → `never`
