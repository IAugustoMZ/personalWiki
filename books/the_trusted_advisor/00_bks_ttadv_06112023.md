# The Trusted Advisor

- trusted advisor relationships $\to$ mentor, coaches, people and leadership
  - today $\to$ horizontal business relationships
    - leadership $\to$ less competences, but more ability to influence, which is the heart of the trusted advisor relationships
- "trust" $\to$ overlaps with branding or reputation
  - ex.: data privacy and security
- implies risk taking
- interpessoal trust $\to$ a human need to deal with another human at some point
- seek advice $\to$ we want accountability ("who is behind this?")
- fewer human interactions, more weight put on the remaining
- *trustworthiness* $\times$ *willingness to trust*
  - not easy to determine
  - trust increases when the "truster" takes a risk by trusting the "trustee", and this one proves to be trustworthy
    - to build trust, play the proactice, risk-taking role of a truster
- human dynamics $\to$ constant
